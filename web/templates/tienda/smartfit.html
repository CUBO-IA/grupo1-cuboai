How to measure

  1. Use a soft tape measure and keep your dog standing and relaxed.
  2. Measure directly on your dog and do not pull the tape tight.
  3. Back length: base of the neck to the base of the tail.
  4. Chest girth: widest part of the chest, behind the front legs.
  5. Neck girth: where the collar sits (only when the garment needs it).
  6. Measure twice and use the larger value if they differ.
  7. Choose your unit once; it applies to all lengths. You can correct your measurements at any time before ordering.
